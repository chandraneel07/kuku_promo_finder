## Quality Check & Failure Mode Diagnostic

### Where the Automated Tool Picks Badly
1. **The False Action Hook (Visual vs. Text Disconnect):** The tool detects high-emotion dialogue words ("You betrayed me"), but the camera direction is a slow wide shot with seated characters. Editors must verify physical action in the first 3 seconds.
2. **Context-Blind Lore Traps:** Promos involving complex council paperwork or secondary character politics (e.g. Father Thomas or Lord Sterling's couriers) score well on dialogue intensity but disorient cold TikTok scrollers who lack 30 episodes of backstory.
3. **Acoustic Boundary Clipping:** Screenplay text end-timestamps cut cleanly on punctuation, but spoken dialogue has natural vocal decay and emotional breath beats. Automated rendering requires a +0.40s audio safety padding.
4. **Disjoint Cross-Scene Jumps:** Stitching non-contiguous scenes across disparate locations requires transitional sound design (whoosh/flash) to prevent cold viewers from assuming a streaming playback error.
