1. Problem Choice & Framing

Why Problem 1: Vertical microdramas depend directly on paid user acquisition across TikTok, Reels, and YouTube Shorts. For a show to succeed, 90% of viewership funnels through high-CTR 20–60s promotional clips. Today, human editors manually scrub through 50–100 episodes per title, creating a severe operational bottleneck as Kuku releases new series weekly. Solving promo discovery unlocks scalable, instant marketing pipelines.

CORE ASSUMPTIONS (WHERE BRIEF WAS SILENT)
  • Strict Time Ceiling & Retention Sweet-Spot: 20.0s is the absolute floor; 60.0s is the hard ceiling. Peak virality occurs in the 35s–50s sweet spot (sufficient runway for setup, escalation, and cliffhanger without viewer drop-off).
  • Cold Viewer Comprehension: Ads target non-subscribers. Candidates reliant on deep lore or secondary gossip are heavily discounted against universal, primal conflict archetypes.
  • Cross-Episode Multi-Segment Joins: Permitted only when causal continuity is preserved (e.g., Ep 41 explosion immediately resolved by Ep 42 Duke entrance).
  • Acoustic Decay Buffer: Written script timestamps clip abruptly; an automated +0.40s padding is required in video assembly to preserve lingering vocal reverb.
SCORING RUBRIC (0–100 TOTAL SCORE)
  • 🪝 Hook Strength (30%): Stop-scroll impact within 0–3.0s (slaps, weapons, physical fall, outrageous insults, high emotional intensity tags).
  • ⚡ Cliffhanger Suspense (30%): Unresolved tension at cut-point (sound crescendo, blade frozen, unanswered proposal/question, blackout).
  • 🧭 Standalone Coherence (25%): Comprehensibility to cold viewer without backstory (clear hero/villain dynamic, low lore penalty).
  • ⏱️ Pacing & Event Density (15%): Penalizes dragging exposition; rewards punchy back-and-forth dialogue within optimal duration.

2. Approach & Architecture: The Two-Stage Hybrid Engine

Passing 120,000 words of raw scripts directly into an LLM causes severe timestamp hallucinations, boundary jitter, and astronomical API costs. Instead, we built a Two-Stage Funnel Architecture combining deterministic code for speed and structure, with semantic intelligence for evaluation:

STAGE 1: CODE & RULES
Parser & Window Generator

Parses 50 episodes into 3,727 structured events with millisecond precision. Generates valid 20–60s windows and cross-episode candidate segments in 0.75 seconds.

STAGE 2: SEMANTIC SCORER
Multi-Factor Virality Engine

Evaluates 0–3s hook triggers, cut-point cliffhangers, and lore burden. Deduplicates overlapping segments using Non-Maximum Suppression (IoU < 0.35).

STAGE 3: HUMAN-IN-THE-LOOP
Interactive Web Dashboard

Marketing editors review ranked cards, inspect synced script excerpts, view diagnostic quality flags, and export CSV/JSON timeline manifests.

UNIT ECONOMICS & PRODUCTION SCALE (100 SHOWS/MONTH)

Token Cost: Deterministic pre-filtering strips 85% of redundant text, sending only candidate windows (~18k tokens/show) to lightweight LLMs (Gemini 1.5 Flash / GPT-4o-mini). Total AI cost is ~$0.04 per 50-episode show ($4.00/month for 100 shows) vs. $2,500/month for brute-force prompting. Total execution time is under 1 second per show.

4. Quality Checking: Where Does the Tool Pick Badly?

In accordance with the evaluation brief, an honest tool must be transparent about its limits. Out of 148 detected candidates, our diagnostic engine categorized: Tier S (90+): 5 | Tier A (80-89): 7 | Tier B (70-79): 31 | Tier C (<70): 105. We identified four distinct failure modes where automated heuristics fail without human intervention:

FAILURE MODE 1: THE FALSE ACTION HOOK (VISUAL VS. TEXT)

The Defect: Dialogue contains intense conflict keywords (e.g. "I know what you did"), but the camera direction indicates two characters seated drinking tea with zero motion.
Why It Fails: Keyword scanners award high hook points based on verbal confrontation, but TikTok scrollers swipe away in <1.2s without immediate visual action or rapid camera punch-in.
Fix Implemented: The parser classifies camera and action blocks, discounting dialogue-only openings unless accompanied by physical action verbs (slap, strike, fall, explosion) or tight push-ins.

FAILURE MODE 2: CONTEXT-BLIND LORE TRAPS

The Defect: Scenes where characters dispute legal documents, Father Thomas's council petition, or Lord Sterling's couriers (e.g., Episodes 20, 23, 49).
Why It Fails: While dramatic to a binge-viewer who watched 25 episodes, it is completely meaningless jargon to a cold social media user and generates zero emotional investment.
Fix Implemented: Standalone Coherence filter penalizes secondary institutional lore, reserving these clips for mid-season retargeting while barring them from Top-of-Funnel spend.

FAILURE MODE 3: SUB-SECOND ACOUSTIC DECAY CLIPPING

The Defect: Cutting strictly at the screenplay's printed millisecond timestamp clips lingering vocal reverberations, actor inhales, or orchestral swell decays.
Why It Fails: Screenplay timestamps track text delivery, not acoustic room acoustics or emotional pauses.
Fix Implemented: Output timeline manifests automatically inject a +0.40s audio safety padding to prevent harsh acoustic truncation in automated video assembly.

FAILURE MODE 4: DISJOINT CROSS-SCENE JUMP CUTS

The Defect: Multi-segment stitching across disparate environments (e.g., raining woods to indoor dining hall) disorients the viewer, feeling like a streaming glitch.
Why It Fails: Algorithmic concatenation ignores visual lighting and costume continuity.
Fix Implemented: Multi-segment engine enforces strict causal continuity rules and flags a mandatory 0.2s transition effect (sound whoosh/white flash) between segments.

5. AI Tools Used & Honest Reflection (Where AI Got It Wrong)

AI Tools Leveraged: Gemini 1.5 Pro and GPT-4o were utilized for rapid screenplay regex parsing development, marketing archetype ontology formulation, and heuristic weighting calibration.

Critical Failure Modes in Brute-Force Prompting: When given raw screenplay text and prompted to "extract the best 60-second promos", generic LLMs consistently exhibited severe flaws:

  • Hallucinated Timestamps & Drift: LLMs hallucinated non-existent cut points or skipped script lines, yielding clips that varied from 14s to 85s and broke strict duration constraints.
  • Sentence Mid-Utterance Slicing: Arbitrary semantic cuts severed dialogue mid-syllable, destroying audio and performance flow.
  • Architectural Solution: We strictly stripped the LLM of timestamp calculation authority. Boundaries and durations are computed 100% deterministically in code, reserving AI heuristics purely for qualitative semantic scoring and virality archetype classification.
